Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Project Cancellation by Client

If you cancel a project after work has started:

  • Any advance / booking fee is non-refundable
  • You will be billed for work completed up to the cancellation date
  • Deliverables completed so far may be provided after payment of dues

2. Project Cancellation by Us

We may pause or cancel a project if payment is delayed, requirements are unclear for a long time, or there is a breach of terms. In such cases, fees for completed work remain payable.

3. Refunds

Refunds, if any, are decided case by case. Unused prepaid amounts for work not yet started may be refunded after deducting administrative costs.

4. Rescheduling

Photography / videography shoots can be rescheduled with at least 48 hours notice, subject to availability. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

5. Contact

To cancel or discuss a project: WhatsApp or Contact page.
