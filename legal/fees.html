Fees and Charges

Last updated: August 2026

1. Pricing

Fees depend on project scope, complexity, and timeline. A written quote or proposal is shared before work starts.

2. Payment Schedule

  • Typically 40–50% advance to start
  • Remaining amount on delivery / milestones as agreed
  • Rush projects may include a priority fee

3. Accepted Methods

UPI, bank transfer, and other methods as mutually agreed. International clients may use appropriate transfer options.

4. Late Payment

Work may be paused if invoices remain unpaid beyond the due date. Late fees may apply if stated in the proposal.

5. Extra Work

Features or revisions outside the original scope are charged separately after approval.

6. Taxes

Applicable taxes (if any) will be mentioned in the invoice as per Indian law.

7. Questions

For a custom quote: Contact Us or WhatsApp.
